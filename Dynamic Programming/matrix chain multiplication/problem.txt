The standard Matrix Chain Multiplication (MCM) problem statement corresponding exactly to that recurrence is:

Given a sequence of matrices \(A_1, A_2, \ldots, A_n\), determine the most efficient way to multiply them by choosing the optimal parenthesization.

The matrices must be multiplied in the given order, but because matrix multiplication is associative, the order of parenthesization can be changed.

The objective is to minimize the total number of scalar multiplications required.
